'I am, we are... different by design' is an annual student-led zine
published by the Department of Typography & Graphic Communiucation at the 
University of Reading. This year, the zine was curated, collated and designed 
by Eleanor Denton, Aurelien Flatman, Grace Johnson and Jess Scrivener.

This year's edition focuses on four themes: mental health, menstrual health,
community and inclusion, and student finances. These were the most commonly
raised issues that students in the School of Arts and Communication Design
highlighted to us in a survey.

In the University's 100th year we have brought together and presented
under-represented student voices from across the school.
